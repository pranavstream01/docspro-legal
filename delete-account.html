Delete your Docs Pro account

For the Docs Pro Android app · Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can delete your Docs Pro account from inside the app. This removes your Docs Pro account from Firebase Authentication and clears Docs Pro's local app data on that device.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Docs Pro and sign in to the account you want to remove, if prompted.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm the on-screen prompts.

If you cannot access the app

Email pranavartiste@gmail.com with the subject “Docs Pro account deletion”. Include the Google sign-in email for the Docs Pro account so we can identify it. Do not send your password, verification codes, identity documents or private documents.

Email an account deletion request

What deletion removes

  • The Firebase Authentication account used to sign in to Docs Pro.
  • Docs Pro documents and related data held in the app's local storage on the device where you delete the account.

What you may need to remove separately

  • Exported backup files or copies you previously saved or shared. Delete them from your Downloads folder, file manager, cloud storage or the other destination where you placed them.
  • Your Google Account. Deleting a Docs Pro account does not delete your Google Account.
  • Google Play order records and payment information, which are managed by Google Play. Contact Google Play for billing or refund questions.
