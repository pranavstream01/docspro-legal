Privacy Policy

For Docs Pro · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Docs Pro is designed to keep the documents you create or import in local app storage on your device. Docs Pro does not upload your documents to a server operated by the developer.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Docs Pro Android app. The app is published by docspro1711. For privacy questions, contact pranavartiste@gmail.com.

Information processed by Docs Pro

  • Your documents: documents, images or PDFs that you create or choose in the app, along with related names and other document details, are stored in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer's servers.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process account information such as your Google account identifier and the profile details Google provides (for example, name and email), along with authentication and security data such as IP address, app identifiers and technical request information. Sign-in is optional; you can use the app without signing in where that option is available.
  • Advertising: when ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and related technical information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google may use the IP address to estimate general location. The app uses Google's consent tools where required so you can make available privacy choices.
  • One-time purchase: Google Play processes payment details for the lifetime “Remove Ads” purchase. Docs Pro receives purchase information needed to confirm the purchase and enable the feature. The developer does not receive your full card or UPI payment credentials through the app.
  • Backups you export: if you use the app's export or share option, the resulting backup is provided to the destination you select. Exported backups are not encrypted by Docs Pro and are outside the app's local storage; protect them and delete any copies yourself.

How information is used and shared

Docs Pro uses local document information to provide the app's document features. Google provides authentication, advertising and payment services described above. Those services process information under their own privacy terms and may use service providers to operate, secure and improve their services. The developer does not sell your personal information.

Docs Pro does not operate a document upload or account-sync server. If you choose to share or export a file, the destination app or service you choose handles that copy under its own terms.

Storage and retention

Documents and app data remain in local app storage until you delete them in Docs Pro, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Copies saved or shared outside Docs Pro must be deleted from those destinations separately. Google retains information processed by its services under Google's own policies and retention practices.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can skip Google sign-in where guest use is offered.
  • You can use the in-app privacy and consent choices to manage advertising privacy options where available.
  • To delete your Docs Pro account and local app data, use the in-app Settings → Delete account option. If you cannot access the app, follow the instructions on the account deletion page.
  • Delete exported backups separately from the file manager, cloud service or other destination where you saved them.

Security

Docs Pro keeps document files in local app storage and uses Google services for the features described in this policy. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Keep your device protected and take care when exporting or sharing backups.

Children's privacy

Docs Pro does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through an account, contact support so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email pranavartiste@gmail.com.
